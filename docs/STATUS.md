- **"Only 5 of 45 v4 eval witnesses are within `ln 64` while the system grades
  18 behaviours, so the search finds other, easier programs"** (the
  clear-world-v3 (#161) row above, its report and ledger section,
  2026-09-22). The count used raw logit gaps, and the pinned policy is not
  calibrated on flat v4 witnesses. One temperature fitted on the v4 holdout,
  under the machine's legal mask, gives **T = 2.597** (validation CE

  first stage should be segment-reset search"** (my own messages to the

  macro tokens and to v4. It moved the planned first stage to segment-reset
  search. Charging each witness the actual per-step deficit of a base-op policy

  reverses it. Bodies carry 79-92% of the deficit, and the costliest
  sub-search is a body in about 70% of witnesses. Segment-reset is predicted

- **"At world-v3's median length the per-step budget is 0.028 nats against a

  authored witness as difficulty of a behaviour: the same system solves 180 of

- **"Quadrupling the beam adds verified behaviours and loses none"** (the width
  row above, from [solved-program-cache (#151)](../../issues/151), 2026-09-19).
  True of the eight cells it measured (four widths, cache on and off) and stated without that scope. Verified behaviours
  are not monotone in beam width: on the staged checkpoint that
  [best-system-command (#158)](../../issues/158) pins, the ladder runs
  16 / 16 / **18** / 17 library-off and 16 / 16 / 16 / 17 library-on.
  `all_positive` is verified at beam 64 and 128 in both arms, lost at 256
  library-on and at 512 library-off; the library-on count is flat at 16 because
  `pair_floor_averages` takes its place. The mechanism is in the search itself
  (`stack_beam.py:334`): the beam returns the
  **first** fitting successor it meets while expanding a layer, before the layer
  is scored at all, so a wider beam
  enlarges the fitting set at every depth and can reach a shortcut that sits
  above the genuine program. `all_positive`'s shortcut is 22 ops against a
  genuine 23. #151's numbers stand; the generalisation does not.
